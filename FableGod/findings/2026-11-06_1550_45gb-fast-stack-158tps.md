# WITHDRAWN: the 45 GB FAST stack's 157.9 tok/s on 12 HumanEval included CD16, which failed quality on MBPP
- Date: 2026-11-06 15:3x-16:00 EET. Written at 15:5x as a headline result. Withdrawn at 16:0x, before it was published,
  when CD16's MBPP quality check failed. Kept as a record of what was measured and why it does not count.
- What was measured (BigMoE, quiet lock, C 224, palindrome, `data/fday/fday_report.txt`, commit b3b1e68):
  - The stack decoded 157.9 tok/s, geometric mean over 12 HumanEval prompts (passes 157.2 / 158.1). Kept 12.58 per
    16-row check. Median check 68.8 ms; cycle ~80 ms.
  - The stack: FAST-NAX experts + DN-KS (per-shape K split for the narrow dense GEMVs) + the lazy GDN commit + CD16 +
    att_single (single-call attention) + the drafter's 17-row block on qmm16c (D7BNAX_32).
- Why it does not count:
  - CD16 keeps the drafter's comment and docstring text without the 125B checking it; code tokens stay checked.
  - On 12 MBPP tasks (BigMoE commit c966c0d, Fable GOAL's pre-registered line), CD16 passed on speed: kept x1.45,
    tok/s +23.7 %.
  - It failed on quality. The tasks' tests passed on 8 of 12 with CD16 against 11 of 12 without it, in both passes:
    3 flips against a line of 2. The answers show comment loops, and code that drifts after the drafter's own
    comments. HumanEval did not show this, because its docstrings copy the prompt.
  - CD16 is dropped. The valid 45 GB FAST code number stays 113.9 tok/s (FAST-NAX alone, same 12 prompts and C,
    `data/fnx/fnx_report.txt`), pending its quality gate. Tonight measures the stack without CD16.
- Also measured in the same run, both KILLed: never-wait -2.8 % (its late work added ~6.5 ms per check) and split 0.99
  on FAST-NAX -1.3 % (kept -5.7 %).
- What the 250-330 tok/s target needs: at kept ~10.4 (the stack without CD16), 250 tok/s needs a ~42 ms cycle. The
  levers priced so far reach ~58-63 ms. The rest needs far fewer expert bytes per check, or more kept per check from a
  drafter we do not have yet.
- How: BigMoE `data/fday/` (b3b1e68, c966c0d); NewIdeas QUEUE 7e81dd1 (the 157.9 entry) and 6525e09 (its correction).
