## 4. What EARLYTAP must deliver [P]
For A5 + D on code: the gain is +13.8 % expected, +6.9 % halved; kept enters tok/s one for one, so the design nets >= +3 % only at kept >= 0.965x, breaks even at 0.935x, and loses below. Cloud's line (>= 0.97x) is the right one. Beyond kept: (1) the drafter's cf must rank first-miss probabilities as well as DFlash2's (ANCHORCOV re-run on EARLYTAP's walk: coverage >= 0.75 at N = 3 on code and chat); (2) its next two choices at its own miss rows must hold the 125B's token as DFlash2's do, 0.69 on code and 0.40 on ifeval (lattice_rescue.py on its lattice: store the selector-ordered top-3 at every tested position); (3) for chat, kept with taps <= 15 (the window), else chat stays on D. Tuesday's report line that reads straight into the plan: `kept(taps <= 24) / kept(DFlash2) on code and chat; ANCHORCOV N = 3 on its cf; top-3 rescue at its miss rows`.

## 5. Both tracks [P]
The draft is exact by construction in both. The FAST levers change the numbers, not the choice: the gate (top-2 experts on sure rows: pairs x0.48) shortens the check (code 33 -> ~25 ms), so the draft's share rises (6.3 of 36: 17 %) and A5's window shrinks (layers 25-48 ~ 12 ms: 3 candidates, or taps <= 20); R2 acceptance raises the all-accepted share (code 0.29 -> ~0.36 at kept x1.06-1.08), which lifts the usable share by ~0.05 and the design's gain by ~1 %. Chat under FAST (R2: kept x1.1-1.2) stays on D: its check stays ~21-23 ms.
