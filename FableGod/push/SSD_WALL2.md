## 0. Verdict in seven lines
1. **The SSD's 42 ms per check at C 256 is two walls, not one [K fit, section 1]:** ~19-22 ms is a FIXED cost per loader call with misses (0.28 ms x ~70-77 calls: GCD wake + the SSD's first byte at QD 1 + the idle-gap wake + the spin's release pickup) and ~21-24 ms is bytes at 11.4-12.7 GB/s. Six prompts, two parameters, residuals <= 4 ms. A bigger cache shrinks only the bytes: at C 320 the fixed part is already half of the SSD time (12 of ~24 ms [P]), at 350's 29 ms cycle it alone would be 13-22 ms.
2. **Exposed today: 0.56 x busy = ~24 ms per check (25 % of the 96.8 ms cycle) [K FIXED-PART], hidden per layer only up to OOO's resident compute (~0.7-0.9 ms at 16 rows, ~0.5 at the live ~10 rows, ~0.25 at chat's 4 rows).** A replay of the per-layer bursts (exposure_replay.py: cap45's captured ids under an LRU at M-17's C 256 sizes, the fitted burst model, a 0.5 ms cover) gives **busy 44.3 and exposed 22.3 ms per check with nothing fitted, against the measured 42.0 and 23.5 [K]**; the per-layer misses are flat (5.5-9.8 per layer: M-17 equalises them), so the exposure is every layer's burst (2 x 0.28 + bytes = 0.86 ms) overflowing its cover by a third, not a few heavy layers.
3. **The floor (section 2):** 350 at kept 12.6 is a 36 ms cycle = at most 421 MB per check from the SSD even when fully hidden = **33 MB per kept token** [P]. The best EXACT design at the 45 GB line holds ~40-46 MB (joint sizes +2.07 GB, the pack, 1.5x sidecar, kept 13.5, the history budget; halved) -> **exact 350 by day is closed on bytes (cap ~250-275 tok/s) even with the SSD fully hidden**; at night (52.6 GB) 31.7 MB fits with zero margin, and then the GPU floor (55 ms per check) is the wall. **FAST fits by day** (Q1-UNION 0.9 at 44.1 GB: 32.6 MB; with the sidecar drop 21.1). At 24 GB: EXACT ~125 MB (cap ~94 tok/s), the best FAST stack 53-72 MB (cap 160-220): **350 at 24 GB is out of reach on bytes on both tracks** even at perfect hiding; the 24 GB line's SSD time can be hidden, not removed.
4. **Design 1, ZERO-LATENCY BURSTS (exact; I/O scheduling, outside caching and prefetch):** one burst per layer (JOINT, built, +7-8 % at 24 GB, never run at 45 GB), the 4 KB / 50 us keepalive (built), demand records split into 8 parallel >= 256 KB pieces at low queue depth (the lone-read latency 0.33 -> ~0.15 ms), the spin's release pickup. Replay: busy 44 -> 26, **exposed 22 -> 8 ms per check (JOINT alone 22 -> 13); halved -7 ms exposed (+8 % tok/s by day, more on chat), and at the 29 ms cycle it is the difference between 13-22 ms of latency and ~5.**
5. **Design 2, THE QUEUE NEVER EMPTIES (exact; adapted prefetch, the pre-route on the partial sum into the volatile tier):** L+1's forecast reads join L's open burst (no new wake, no first byte), so the SSD sees one stream per check instead of ~75 cold bursts, and the demand burst at L+1 carries only the forecast's misses. If tonight's capture passes its line (recall >= 0.85 at precision >= 0.80), the replay gives exposed 22 -> 7 ms at C 256 alone and **22 -> 1.9 ms with design 1 (2.2 even at the L-input forecast's recall 0.5)**: **the exposed SSD time vanishes at 45 GB** (halved: -10 ms per check, +12 % by day); the bytes do not.
6. **Design 3, FEWER BYTES EXACT (compression and pools; outside prefetch):** the records3m M-01 pack already on disk (-4.8 % bytes, bitwise, the 45 GB preset reads the unpacked records3), the 11-bit W24-sp pack (-10.9 %, needs a 63 GB copy: Kamen's disk call), the entropy floor of the codes (-9 %, KILL on decode cost), 1.5x sidecar slots (+2.1 GB, -2.4 MB halved), joint sizes (+2.07 GB -12 %), the acceptance-history prefetch budget (-1 MB). Together on the 45 GB line: 47.6 -> ~37-40 MB halved; still above 33. **The FAST stack is what crosses 33 by day: U3 (Q1-UNION 0.9 + 3-bit sure rows, built, smoke tonight) x0.54 of live bytes, the sidecar drop on top x0.65.**
7. **Pre-registered screens (section 5):** the trace's per-burst fit (PASS = fixed >= 0.20 ms per burst and >= 35 % of the SSD time at C 224/256: design 1 is worth building first), JOINT's live delta (c45t vs c45tj: PASS -8 ms of SSD busy per check), the keepalive A/B (ka0 / ka1: BigMoE's +2 % line), the pre-route's recall (p3 >= 0.85), a 10-min SSD microbench for the pieces (lone 2.16 MB read vs 8 x 270 KB in parallel after 0.8 ms gaps: PASS -0.10 ms), and a CPU exposure replay of the stall trace (Σ max(0, burst - cover) with the issue moved one layer early: PASS exposed <= 8 ms per check).

**What the 0.28 ms per call is made of [K probes; G shares]:** the SSD's first byte at QD 1 (~0.09 ms: a lone 2.2 MB read takes 296 us = 206 us of bytes at 10.7 GB/s + ~90 us [K M-20]; 331 us for 2.9 MB [K B1]); the idle-gap wake (+50-65 us after 0.25-1 ms gaps [K M-20]; the engine's gaps between a layer's two rows and between layers are 0.3-1 ms at C 256 [P from 84 ms / 48 layers], so nearly every call pays it: ~4.5 ms per check); GCD wake + the spin's release pickup (the sync-free gate polls GPU-written ids; SC5 measured +35-130 us of poll overshoot after 0.6-2 ms stalls [K LEDGER]; ~0.1 ms [G]); the burst tail (the last read of a burst of 2-8 parallel reads ends ~0.03-0.05 ms after the mean [G]). **Exposed vs hidden [K / G]:** Breakthrough's FIXED-PART fit (0.56 x demand SSD ms in the steady fixed cost) says 44 % of the busy time is hidden. The mechanism is per layer: OOO computes the resident experts while the loader serves the layer's two rows; a burst is hidden up to that cover (NAX6 43 ms / 48 = 0.9 ms per layer at 16 rows, 3c 1.05; x the resident share ~0.8 -> 0.7-0.85 ms; chat's 4 rows ~0.25 ms). Today's median layer burst = 2 x 0.28 + 3.4 MB / 12.7 = 0.83 ms [P]: the fixed part alone fills two thirds of the cover. So the exposure is Σ_layers max(0, burst - cover): shortening the FIXED part shortens every burst and empties the exposure faster than cutting bytes does (a 1 ms bytes cut spread over 48 layers removes 0.02 ms per burst; one row pairing removes 0.28).
**Chat [K day9 / replay]:** at C 256 chat checks hold 3-4 rows and miss ~100-150 experts per check [G from C 160's 275-1,133 at kept 2-3]; the cover is 0.25 ms per layer, so chat's bursts are ~all exposed: the per-call latency (~20 ms) is most of chat's SSD time and most of its cycle's SSD exposure; design 1 is worth more on chat than on code in relative terms (chat's cycle ~50-60 ms).

## 3. Three designs to remove the exposed SSD time (deliverable 2), the outside-caching one first (deliverable 3)
### D1 ZERO-LATENCY BURSTS (EXACT; I/O scheduling: outside caching and prefetch). Prior art: adapted (SlotStream's batched sorted submits = our SSD1, PARKED after only the alignment item; NVMe queue-depth practice; our own JOINT / M-20 / REC2 measurements). New here: the per-call fixed cost as the day-side SSD wall at >= C 256, and the pieces step, which reverses REC2 at low queue depth.
- **What:** (a) **JOINT at 45 GB** (BIGMOE_JOINT=1: the sidecar row's reads start with the base row's, one burst per layer; bitwise; +7-8 % at 24 GB; tonight's c45tj_224 is its first 45 GB run): calls with misses ~77 -> ~45 per check. (b) **Keepalive** (BIGMOE_SSD_KEEPALIVE_US=50, built: ~80 MB/s of 4 KB reads while decoding; removes the +50-65 us wake entirely [K M-20]; tonight's ka1). (c) **Pieces at low queue depth:** a demand record (base 2.16 MB, sidecar 0.77) is one preadv today; split it into 8 (base) / 3 (sidecar) parallel preads of >= 256 KB (the size above which the drive keeps >= 98 % of peak [K B1]) so a layer with 1-3 misses runs at QD 8-24 instead of 1-3: a lone read's 0.33 ms -> ~0.15 [K TRIED 148: 9 pieces 0.28 vs 0.40 for 2.77 MB; B1: 256 KB reads 13.55 GB/s]; the ext's 9-destination preadv path already exists, this is the reverse flag. (d) **Release pickup:** poll the GPU's ids with a tighter spin where the stall is short (SC5's knobs; no gain at 24 GB where stalls were 2 ms, re-test at 45 GB where they are 0.5-0.9 ms [G]).
- **Physics budget at C 256 (median check) [P from the fit; replay]:** (a) JOINT: calls 83 -> 46 per check, -0.28 x 37 = -10 ms busy, exposed 22.3 -> 12.6 [P replay]; (b) keepalive -0.06 x 46 = -2.7; (c) pieces -0.10 x ~30 lone-read layers = -3; (d) release pickup -0.05 x 46 = -2 [G]; (b)-(d) together take the per-call cost to ~0.10: busy 26, exposed 8.4 [P replay]. **Bound -18 ms busy / -14 ms exposed per check, halved -7 ms exposed = +8 % tok/s by day on code; chat (cover 0.25): exposed 33 -> 15 in the replay's units, i.e. about half of chat's SSD exposure [P].** At C 320 (night): busy 24 -> 15 bound, the fixed share 50 % -> 30 %. **At the 29-36 ms cycle:** the fixed 13-22 ms per check becomes ~5: without D1 the cycle cannot hold the latency even with the bytes at 33 MB.
- **Risk:** the per-call cost may sit on the CPU side (GCD wake, Python's call post) rather than in the drive: then (b) and (c) gain nothing and only (a) stands (-9 bound / -4.5 halved). The trace's per-burst fit separates them (section 5). Pieces cost CPU (8 preads instead of 1) and 8x the completions: at 24 GB this is why REC2 merged them; at 45 GB there are 171 misses per check, 1,400 preads per check = ~1 % of a core [G].
- **Screen (<= 10 min, SSD + CPU, no GPU, any BigMoE / MoonShots session; ssdwake.py's harness):** 40 rounds x 45 bursts with 0.8 ms gaps, arms: 1 lone 2.16 MB read; 8 x 270 KB parallel; base + sidecar sequential (2 bursts) vs joint (1 burst, 11 pieces); each with and without the 4 KB / 50 us keepalive. **PASS:** pieces cut the lone burst by >= 0.10 ms AND joint by >= 0.20 ms per layer; **KILL:** < 0.04 / < 0.10. Prediction [G]: pieces -0.12, joint -0.25, keepalive -0.06: PASS 65 %.
