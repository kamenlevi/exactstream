## Why

The drafter already proposes the right token. DFlash2's top-3 holds the 125B's token 89 % of the time at mid rows [K] (KWALL (a), MTP Head, 11-03).

But a verified row costs ~7.82 ms at 45 GB [K], so every way of using the top-3 prices out (MTP Head's TOP3-USE, DEPTH32 and DIVERSE2):

| Idea | Result |
|---|---|
| Tree of top-3 | -10 .. +2 % |
| Learned picker | +0.3 % |
| Per-depth DFlash2 + MTP pick | x0.92 |
| 32-row drafts | x0.66 |
| Ensembles | +0.3-1 % |

The lever is the row cost.

Today also showed that cheap passes can certify, on the 12 GB two-pass check [K]:
- pass 1 at top-1 + union members (P1-K1) matched top-2: 7/2997 certified flips vs 7/3019;
- pass 1 at 3 bits: certified flips 4/2970 vs 8/2976.
