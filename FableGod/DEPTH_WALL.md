## 0. Verdict in five lines
1. **The decay is the drafter's own, calibrated uncertainty rising with depth, not stale taps and not the 125B's mid rows** [K]. On code the accuracy within each cf band is the same at every depth (cf ≥ 0.999: 100 % at depths 1-4 and 13-15; 0.99-0.999: 100 → 99.4; 0.9-0.99: 92.0 → 93.4; < 0.9: 60.5 → 59.1), while the share of positions at cf ≥ 0.999 falls 62 → 21 % and at cf < 0.9 rises 16 → 25 %. Re-weighting the deep band with the shallow band's cf mix gives 92.7 % (the measured shallow 92.9); re-weighting the shallow band with the deep mix gives 88.0 % (the measured deep 87.9). Math is the same shape (88.8 → 80.4; cf ≥ 0.999 42 → 14 %, cf < 0.9 22 → 39 %).
2. **The deep misses sit at 125B-sure rows, increasingly inside copies and on numbers** [K]: at depths 13-15 the classed positions are 96 % sure and the drafter is right 88.4 % there (98.6 % at depths 1-4); 78 % of deep positions are inside ≥ 8-token copies, where it drops to 89.6 % (99.6 % shallow); numbers go from 2 % to 14 % of misses on code and 4 → 27 % on math; the mid rows' share falls 21 → 4 %. The drafter loses track of what it is copying and counting, and knows it (its cf falls).
3. **The copy override flattens the copy part completely** [K real walks]: at 32 rows the per-depth rates beyond 15 go from 0.82 / 0.79 / 0.83 / 0.81 / 0.78 / 0.67 to 0.95 / 0.94 / 0.99 / 0.98 / 0.98 / 0.99 and 0.98-1.00 to depth 31; kept per 32 rows 10.26 → 12.35, which is the kept a flat drafter at the shallow rate would give (a flat 0.935 keeps 12.7 of 32 [P]). On HumanEval+ the depth wall is already solved by the override; on copy-poor code (MBPP: 5 % copies) it stands and the override does nothing (×0.98-1.00).
4. **Per-depth cf thresholds, depth-weighted losses and line-start anchoring have nothing to give** [K]: the calibration is depth-invariant (why CALSTOP's per-depth stop rules added nothing and the flat t 0.3 is right); deep positions are not miscalibrated but uninformed, so loss weights by depth (SD1 / R-05: 0.97×) and emphasis on hard positions (HPD-1: KILL) cannot add information; anchoring at a line start changes nothing deep (85.7 vs 88.0 %) and +4 points shallow by selection (lines start with easy tokens).
5. **The best kept per 32-row check on code, calibrated**: 12.35 with the override (real) → ~13.7 with the pick at today's overlap → ~15-16 with the AR-flat chain re-processing the deep half (the trained MTP head holds 0.94 flat [K D-22], blended with the override's 0.95-1.00 on copies) — and that chain pays only at ≤ 1 ms per step. **20 per 32 is not a depth problem**: a drafter with no decay at today's shallow rate keeps 12.7; 20 needs 0.975 per position flat, i.e. the shallow rate itself (mid rows at 66 %, knowledge misses at sure rows) must rise to the near-copy's level, which is DRAFTER_98's knowledge wall.
