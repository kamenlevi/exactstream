# MoE-Spec: Expert Budgeting for Efficient Speculative Decoding (arXiv 2602.16052, 17 Feb 2026; McDanel, Li, Surineni, Khaitan)
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16052 ; HTML https://arxiv.org/html/2602.16052v1. Source: WebSearch extracts [S].
- What: training-free, verification-time. Given the draft tree, sum routing probabilities over all draft tokens per layer, keep the top-B experts (a per-layer shortlist); tokens whose natural experts fall outside the shortlist are truncated (or substituted). The budget caps expert loading per layer so the cost stops growing with tree size.
- Numbers [S]: 10-30 % higher throughput than EAGLE-3 at comparable quality; B is a tunable accuracy / latency knob; the gain is largest for single-request inference where expert loading dominates. Reviews note limits with batching and non-softmax routers. Accuracy deltas per B not in the extracts (AcceptMoE's -0.27 points vs EAGLE-3 is a different paper).
- Maps to us: a per-check expert cap by summed router mass = TRIED "Per-pass expert budget b16" (BETWEEN at 24 GB: +25.8 % tok/s, over the strict KLD on GSM / IFEval; f24b16 PASS vs its exact mode; B12LL-b16 KILL at 12 GB; M-09 CONTRIB16 KILL: contribution norm no better than router mass). Our data says mass-ranked budgets hit mid rows (the shortlist drops a mid row's rank-0/1 experts when the block's mass is elsewhere), which is why Q1 (per-row, by the drafter's cf, rank 0-1 always kept) passes where the block budget is BETWEEN.
- TRIED status: b16 family BETWEEN / KILL; the row-selective form (Q1 / QOM) PASS.
- Open: MoE-Spec's truncation vs substitution numbers per B on Qwen-class 512-expert models; whether they protect each token's rank-0.
