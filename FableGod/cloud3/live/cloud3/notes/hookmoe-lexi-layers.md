# Per-layer expert counts: HookMoE (EMNLP 2025, Cheng et al.), LExI (arXiv 2509.02753), Yang et al. ACL Findings 2026 (ASET)
- Links: https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1610 ; code https://github.com/KerwinKai/HookMoE ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02753 ; https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.2140. Source: WebSearch extracts [S].
- HookMoE: "Top Layer Needs Less Experts" (TLNLE): post-trained MoEs can activate fewer experts in their upper layers; the layers most sensitive to the expert count sit near the MIDDLE; a trainable Hook module (one layer, small calibration set) compensates: four MoE models, ~2.5 % average degradation at > 50 % fewer activated experts, 1.42x prefill.
- LExI: per-layer static k by a data-free perturbation profile + evolutionary search under a total budget; Qwen1.5-MoE +10 % accuracy at equal throughput vs pruning (another summary: +5.1 % throughput).
- Yang et al.: static layer-wise top-k schedules found by search: 10-78 % throughput at "minimal" loss; the best DeepSeek-V2-Lite schedule does NOT transfer to DeepSeek-V3 and vice versa; ASET adds token-wise uncertainty adaptivity (OLMoE Pareto curve).
- Maps to us: the per-layer sensitivity profile of THIS model under FEWER experts is unmeasured; what is measured is the REUSE profile (DM P-frame): layers 8-11 free, 12-35 cheap (0.10-0.14 % per 12-layer half on sure rows), 0-7 and 36-47 expensive (19-23 %) -- the opposite shape of HookMoE's "middle most sensitive", so their schedule must not be copied. A per-layer k on mid rows is the untested arm: mid rows keep 10 in layers 0-7 / 36-47 and k_mid in 8-35; priced on the routing data in P2 (union per layer band), quality by one map arm (REQUESTS).
- TRIED status: per-layer pool SIZES (M-17) adopted; per-layer k: untried.
