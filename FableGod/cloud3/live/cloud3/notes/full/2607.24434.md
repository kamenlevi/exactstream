## 2. Mechanism (precisely)
- Cost model: a block's expert set U_l(B) = union over its tokens of the per-layer TopK sets; cost C(B) = sum over layers and experts in U_l(B) of c_{l,e} "where c_{l,e} captures expert loading and execution" (eq. 2, l.66-72). "The central bottleneck is expert-set expansion: each additional token may introduce newly loaded experts" (l.72-74). The unit of merit becomes "accepted tokens per expert-set expansion" (l.47-48).
- Fixed-footprint drafter (eq. 5, l.358-360): y_l^d = S_l(h_l) + g_{l,e1} E_{l,e1}(h_l) + gamma_l E_l^d(h_l), e1 = argmax of the target router. One added draft expert E_l^d per MoE layer, accelerator-resident; "Only the draft experts and optional scales are trainable" (l.362); the target MoE is frozen. Draft-expert size/architecture: not stated.
- Self-distillation (l.320-365), teacher-forced on the frozen target: residual loss L_res = sum ||gamma_l E_l^d(h^d) - m*||^2 with m* = y* - S_l(h*) - g_{e1} E_{e1}(h*), i.e. the routed-expert contribution that top-1 drops (eq. 6-7, l.327-333); logit KL at temperature T (eq. 8, l.338); token CE against the target argmax (eq. 9, l.347); router-agreement KL(R_l(h*) || R_l(h^d)) (eq. 10, l.352-356). L = L_logit + alpha L_tok + beta L_res + eta L_router (eq. 11, l.362). alpha, beta, eta, T, corpus, steps: not stated. "All supervision comes from unlabeled text and the frozen target MoE" (l.364-365).
- Confidence-expansion truncation (l.409-429): q_i = "a confidence score from the draft logits"; E^_{l,i} = verifier experts predicted by the draft router for layer l; predicted block set U^_i = union_{j<=i} union_l E^_{l,j} (eq. 12, l.415); marginal expansion Delta_i = |U^_i| - |U^_{i-1}| (eq. 13, l.420); stop before appending token i iff q_i < tau AND Delta_i > B_i (eq. 14, l.424). "B_i can be a fixed layer-expert budget or a budget derived from the current expert cache" (l.426-427). Values of tau, B_i, K_max: not stated. Exact because "discarded candidates are never emitted, and all emitted candidates are verified by the target MoE" (l.427-429).
- Draft-router prefetch (l.431-437; Algorithm 1 l.438-467): as soon as token i is kept, "non-blocking prefetches for newly predicted verifier experts E^_{l,i} that are not already cached or in flight" (Alg. line 13: prefetch U^_i \ (C u P)); transfers overlap the remaining draft computation; "verification only waits for unfinished predicted transfers", misses are loaded on demand (l.437, 462). Unused prefetch = "wasted bandwidth" (l.412). The router loss exists because it "directly affects prefetch hit rate and wasted movement" (l.414-415).

## 3. Setup
- Targets: DeepSeek-V2-Lite (DS) and Moonlight-16B-A3B (ML); "each use 27 layers, 64 routed + 2 shared experts, and top-6 active routed experts" (l.458-460). DS: routed experts 14.4B params = 91.6% of weights, 26.8 GiB in BF16; all non-routed weights 2.4 GiB; top-6/64 = 9.4% of the routed pool; 15.7B total, 2.45B active/token (l.97-109, 136-149).
- CG platform: RTX 4090 24 GB, BF16 PyTorch 2.11.0+cu128, CPU-resident routed experts copied to GPU on demand (l.440-442). MN platform: Xiaomi MIX Flip 2, Snapdragon 8 Elite, Hexagon HTP v81 NPU ("about 73/146 TOPS" INT8/INT4, "about 8 MB on-chip SRAM"), llama.cpp with Q4_0 weights, Flash-resident experts staged into shared CPU/GPU/NPU memory (l.444-455). No persistent accelerator expert cache unless an experiment says so (l.473-475).
- Expert loading share of decode-128 profiled time: 87% CPU->GPU, 49% Flash->NPU; the NPU figure "excludes runtime API gap" (Fig. 1b, l.137-159).
- Prompts: "Chat, GSM8K, MBPP, summarization, and instruction following" (l.456-457). Baselines: AR offload; shared+top-r, "the strongest training-free self-drafting baseline after sweeping r in {1, 2, 3}"; DE = DraftExpert with truncation + prefetch (l.477-490). TPS = decode-stage after prefill; speedup normalised to AR expert-offloaded decoding on the same model/platform (l.424-427, right column). Single-user, latency-critical, no batching (l.14-17).

## 5. Limitations
- Stated: "the final throughput gain is moderate rather than equal to the full resident-memory upper bound. DraftExpert reduces and overlaps expert movement, but exact verification still exposes misses, prefetch waste, and non-expert runtime overhead" (l.554-558); gains "intentionally not uniform across platforms" (l.495-496); 12-14% of prefetched experts are wasted (Table 4).
- Not stated: tau, B_i, K_max, loss weights, training corpus/steps/cost, draft-expert size, confidence definition, per-benchmark results, variance. Only 16B-class targets (64 experts, top-6); nothing on larger MoEs, batching, or SSD-resident experts on a PC. Single author, no affiliation, v1.
