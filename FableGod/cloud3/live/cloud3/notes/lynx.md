# Lynx: batch-aware expert selection (arXiv 2411.08982; v1 Nov 2024 "Dynamic Batch-Aware Expert Selection", v2 "Efficient MoE Inference Through Dynamic, Workload-agnostic Expert Reduction")
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.08982v2 (HTML v1: https://arxiv.org/html/2411.08982v1). Source for this note: WebSearch extracts [S].
- What: at batch granularity, reduce the number of DISTINCT experts a batch activates by remapping some token -> expert assignments at runtime, training-free, no calibration data. Three observations: router confidence marks the mappings that matter; primary (rank-0) choices matter much more than secondary; decode is resilient, prefill is sensitive.
- Numbers [S]: up to 1.55x speedup (GSM8K, HumanEval) on Mixtral-class models; boosts existing techniques up to 1.38x; vs NAEE on GSM8K keeps > 55 % accuracy at 1.5x where NAEE drops below 40 %. Batch sizes and exact accuracy deltas not in the extracts.
- Maps to us: our "batch" is one 16-row verify check (plus the bonus row). The rank-0-matters finding = our Q1 / TOP1-QOM (sure rows keep rank 0-1). The remap (secondary experts of a token moved to experts the batch already loads) is the lossy part: TRIED I6 / REROUTE b25 found re-routing dominated by simple dropping (experts orthogonal, |cos| 0.024), and OEA's form (keep top-k0, ADD union members, never substitute) is what passed here. Lynx's decode-vs-prefill resilience matches our sure-vs-mid row split only loosely: Lynx keys on router confidence, we key on the drafter's cf (better calibrated for the verify rows).
- TRIED status: adapted form in use (UNION-STACK / Q1-UNION: OEA-style). Substitution form: KILL (I6, REROUTE b25, SERE-style fallback).
- Open: does Lynx drop or substitute? (v2 "expert reduction" suggests dropping low-confidence secondary mappings and re-normalising) - if it re-normalises, our M-09c says no-renorm is better here. Their per-layer sensitivity curve would price a per-layer k0.
