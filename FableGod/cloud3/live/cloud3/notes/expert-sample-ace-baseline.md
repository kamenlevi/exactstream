# Uniform k reduction at inference: Expert-Sample (arXiv 2602.02443, Feb 2026, v2 May 2026), ACE (arXiv 2609.05228, Sep 2026, Xu et al.), "Don't Skip the Baseline" (NeurIPS 2026, Steimle et al.), MoDES (CVPR 2026)
- Links: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02443 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05228 ; https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/160490 ; https://cvpr.thecvf.com/virtual/2026/poster/37515. Source: WebSearch extracts [S].
- Expert-Sample ("Certain Head, Uncertain Tail"): on five fine-grained MoEs (Qwen3 Instruct variants incl. Qwen3-30B-A3B, default top-8) GREEDY accuracy stays stable down to half the default top-k (Fig. 2a), while pass@n degrades; keeps the top floor(k/2)+1 experts deterministically and samples the rest from a wider rank range (test-time scaling, not speed).
- ACE: training-free, calibration-free token-adaptive skipping inside the router's own top-k candidates (a global spectral proxy x a router-conditioned refinement; always keeps top-1); at 50 % skipping on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B: WikiText-2 ppl -7.96 % and +4.15 points avg downstream vs the strongest competitor; Gemma-4-26B-A4B at 50 % skip 79.51 vs RCR 77.49, at 60 % 77.11 vs 68.96; one table shows BF16 81.63 vs ACE 72.11 (ratio unknown): i.e. 50 % skipping still costs several points on some models.
- Don't Skip the Baseline: a controlled comparison of four dynamic-skipping methods against simply reducing k statically on all layers and tokens; the baseline is hard to beat, the dynamic gains are minor; calibration-time effective k does not match test-time k.
- Maps to us: "greedy accuracy stable at half top-k" is a benchmark-level claim at top-8 -> top-4 on 30B-class models; on this 125B the strict gates say otherwise: L1-17c top-6 = 2.98x, top-4 = 9.70x the 3-bit tier's error; S9 / M-09d top-8 renormalised KLD 124-145 % of the strict gate; the map: topk5 flips 8.6-14.5 % of mid rows (0.00 % of sure rows). Benchmark scores may still hold (TK1 top-8 passed the Eval suite at 12 GB, 5/5), which is exactly the FAST quality rule, so "uniform top-8" is a legal FAST lever here at union x0.84 (16-row union 74.8 -> 62.4, -16 %) and top-5 everywhere would be the Expert-Sample claim: 16-row union x0.59 [P FLOOR_125B 2.4: 4,913 / 8,366 MB] at a flip rate (1.6 % code all-row, 11.5 % chat) that is PASS on code by the map line (1.34 %? no: 1.60 > 1.34, BETWEEN) and KILL on chat.
- TRIED status: uniform top-8 PASS (opt-in, 12 / 24 GB); top-6 / top-5 / top-4 uniform: KILL by the strict gate, BETWEEN on code by the map.
- Open: ACE's "router-conditioned refinement" is a per-token signal from the router's own scores that beats a static cut by +4 points at 50 % skipping; our gate-share threshold test (KILL, "a cliff") used a single threshold. A per-row score-shape rule (skip a slot when its share is < a fraction of the row's top share AND the row's top-2 mass is high) on MID rows only is untested; map arm price ~5 min.
