# MoE + speculative decoding analyses: MoESD (arXiv 2505.19645), Utility-Driven SD / Cascade (2506.20675), DraftExpert (2607.24434), EcoSpec (2607.12696), The Limits of Speculation (2609.22156), MoE-SpeQ (2511.14102), SP-MoE (2510.10302)
- Links: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19645 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20675 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24434 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12696 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22156 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14102 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10302. Source: WebSearch extracts [S] + the project's own notes (NOVELTY.md, BYTES10.md sources).
- MoESD: closed form for the expected activated experts of a t-token batch, N(t) = E (1 - ((E - K) / E)^t) (independent-routing model); argues SD helps MoE more than dense at moderate batch. Check against us [P]: E 512, K 10, t 16 gives 512 (1 - 0.9805^16) = 139, but the measured union is 75-80: rows of one chain are far from independent (they share ~45 % of their experts with the rest of the block). The independence model overstates our union by ~1.8x: any mechanism must be judged on the measured k^0.67 growth, not on this formula.
- Cascade (Utility-driven SD): at low batch, draft tokens activate 2-3x more weights, verification time 2-3x; a utility metric (token gain / verification cost) chooses when to speculate and K; +7-14 % throughput over static K, slowdown capped ~5 %. Here: the t 0.3 running-confidence cut is the same knob and is already at its optimum (CALSTOP / S45d / CUTSWEEP KILL).
- DraftExpert: on-device self-speculative decoding; the union of K draft tokens' experts grows the verify cost ("expansion-aware"); router-agreement distillation for prefetch hit rates 86-88 %. Here: EVICT / DraftExpert style = the t 0.3 truncation (NOVELTY: "t 0.3 truncation (EVICT / DraftExpert)").
- EcoSpec: expert-aware DRAFT selection by predicted marginal expert activation cost (choose drafts that add few new experts). Here: untried as such; M-10 says a sibling row adds 2.35-2.55 new experts per layer, a chain row 2.79: there is little to choose between candidates, and the drafter's top-1 is right 93 % of the time on code, so draft selection by expert cost trades kept tokens for union at a bad rate [G]. Candidate for the P1 table (cheap CPU test exists: pick, at each depth, among the drafter's top-2 the token whose predicted route adds fewer new experts, on traces with top-20 routes).
- The Limits of Speculation (2609.22156): bounds SD speedup in MoE by expert-loading growth with draft length. Here: our measured union curve and kept cap (~13) are the same bound, measured.
- MoE-SpeQ / SP-MoE: draft-model-driven expert prefetch (<= 32 tokens ahead; draft states through the target's routers, 89 % top-1). Here: FAR / FAR-G1 (SSD wall), not a union lever.
- TRIED status: all adapted or measured; EcoSpec's draft-choice form untried.
