# SERE: Similarity-based Expert Re-routing for Efficient Batch Decoding in MoE Models (arXiv 2602.07616, 7 Feb 2026; Wu, Cheng, Lv, Dan, Yuan; ICLR 2026)
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07616 (HTML https://arxiv.org/html/2602.07616v1) ; code https://github.com/JL-Cheng/SERE ; ICLR page https://iclr.cc/virtual/2026/poster/10011142. Source: WebSearch extracts [S].
- What: batch-level dynamic skipping: tokens are re-routed from their SECONDARY experts to the most similar PRIMARY experts (experts already needed as someone's primary in the batch), using expert-similarity patterns; similarity also marks critical experts that are never dropped. Custom CUDA kernel, vLLM one-line integration.
- Numbers [S]: up to 2.0x speedup vs the PyTorch path at QPS 24 on Qwen1.5-MoE with "minimal" quality loss on reasoning benchmarks; per-benchmark deltas not in the extracts.
- Maps to us: re-routing to a SIMILAR expert is exactly what TRIED killed on this model: SERE-style fallback (TRIED quality: "SERE / SPICE / mean-expert fallback: KILL, routed experts mutually orthogonal, best |cos| median 0.024; the best scaled other expert leaves 100 % (median) of the missing energy"), I6 near-tie re-routing (KILL), REROUTE b25 (KILL). Qwen1.5-MoE (60 experts, top-4) has more redundant experts than this 512-expert fine-grained MoE; the similarity premise fails here by measurement.
- TRIED status: KILL (the substitution family).
- Open: SERE's similarity metric is output-based on calibration data; our |cos| was on weights (and "scaled other expert leaves ~100 % of the energy" is output-level): the premise is closed unless a new similarity measure finds pairs this model's router treats as interchangeable (P2 can check co-activation / substitutability at one layer from the traces, no weights needed).
