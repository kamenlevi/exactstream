## What T0 does (Fit12's hook, ~/Fit12/scripts/t0_hook.py)
- Exact AR prefill of prompt[:-1] (bt_b12.b12_prefill).
- Per token, pass 1 uses ranks 0-1 only, at their mass weights. Ranks 2-9 get weight 0 and are never read.
- The token is kept if pass 1's max softmax >= theta. Otherwise the row is rolled back and re-run exactly (the one-row
  path, ALLWAIT).
- Greedy only. The MTP head is not loaded.
- Fit12's gates (PLAN.md "T0 gates RESULT"):
  - theta 1.01 equals the exact AR decode bit for bit (tokens and every step's logits);
  - deterministic;
  - pass 1 is top-2 (0 ALLWAIT calls).
