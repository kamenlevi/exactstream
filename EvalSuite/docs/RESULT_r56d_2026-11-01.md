## What it means
- **OOO + the fused combine can join the 45 GB default as an exact change** (NewIdeas' call, as pre-registered: BigMoE's
  R57_PRESET=45).
  - Leap's R-69 shows them bitwise at the tensor level, at 1-16 rows.
  - Leap's R-69b shows them token-equal in the live loop on 4 prompts.
  - R-56d shows them bit-exact at full length: one-token decode and 16-row checks, 36 prompts, against both the arm's
    own AR and REF2-T's AR from R-56c.
- **The port is live for every EM.load run** (Leap's and BigMoE's harnesses included): setting BIGMOE_TIER_COMBINE=1
  (+ BIGMOE_OOO=1) now engages them. With COMBINE off nothing changes.
- **Not covered here:** check widths 2-15. This harness always checks 16 rows; the 45 GB default truncates (L1-14, t 0.3).
  R-69 (tensor, bitwise at 1-16 rows) and R-69b (live, tokens equal) cover them.
