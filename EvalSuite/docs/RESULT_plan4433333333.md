## Caveats
- Prefill tests apply the tiers to every token (conservative); the deployed plan keeps prefill exact.
- The decode check has no accepted-build column (time); the prefill flips (5.7 % vs 8.5 %) predict the accepted build
  would disagree more than the plan, but that is not measured.
- RULER at 16K: 24 items are noisy (same-weights floor 70.8 % vs reference 75.0 %).
- The loop detector (a 20-token n-gram 3x in the last 400 tokens) also flags structured text; item-level reading above.
