## What it means
- **The 45 GB exactness wall is down at full length.** The 16-row live drafting loop is bit-identical to one-token
  decoding under REF2b.
  - On Friday night REF2 failed exactly here: 28/36, with 8 math answers flipping a token at a tie once REF2's AR passed
    512 keys (`docs/RESULT_r56_2026-10-30.md`).
  - Leap's key buckets (REF2b) fixed that. REF2b's reference rows passed decision 4's same-scores test
    (`docs/RESULT_ref2b_2026-10-31.md`), and NewIdeas adopted REF2b as the 45 GB exact reference at 04:5x.
- **BigMoE's gate keeps it.** The gate is the faster exact check: R-55's 2x on warm checks, +35-48 % live. It is
  bit-identical in the live loop at full length too, so the route NewIdeas would adopt (REF2b on the gate) is exact.
- **What it enables:** every speed-up on this route can be checked token for token against REF2b at any context
  length, with R-56's 36 prompts as the standing audit.
