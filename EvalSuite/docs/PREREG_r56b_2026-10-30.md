## Reference: REF2b's own AR, at full length
- The `ref2b` arm's process runs REF2b's AR on each prompt, as the reference generator does it (`ref2.prefill` +
  `ar_step`, PF1 on), to the item's max_tokens. It saves the tokens plus each logits row's sha1 (its bytes, dtype and
  shape) and top-3 to `results/r56b/ar/<key>.json`. The gate arm reads those same files.
- **Reproduction check:** the AR's tokens must equal `results/ref2b`'s row (runner 30). If not, that prompt is VOID:
  the reference doesn't reproduce.

## The loop and the comparison (`evalsuite/live16b.py`, stage `live16b`)
- The loop is live16's (Leap's STEP 3k harness: DFlash2 L1-20, 16-row checks, commit keep k+1, SB), run to
  max_tokens.
- Every kept check row is hashed and compared with the AR row at its position, so every AR row is compared once.
- Every token is compared too. A prompt stops at its first differing token.
- The first row that differs in bits is recorded: position, check, row, rows kept, REF2 path counts, and the top-3
  of both sides.

## Amendment 2 (Fri 20:5x, before any row): a shared lock
- **Why:** this audit is bits-only (tokens and logits rows bitwise; its tok/s is information), so by Kamen's FAST
  INTEGRATION rule and the orchestrator's 20:4x note it runs under `peer lock share` (`cli.gpu --share`), not quiet.
- **How it shares:**
  - The share lock is refused while a quiet lock runs, and asked for again.
  - When a quiet ticket waits, the process stops at the next prompt boundary (live16b's yield, exit 75), and runner 31
    asks again without counting it as a chunk with no rows.
- **Unchanged:** C 192 (the configuration the 45 GB route would adopt) and the 46 GB floor.
- **Speed:** its tok/s is labelled "shared GPU, rough".
- Runner 31 stopped again at 20:43, with no row, when runner 30 stopped. It is relaunched with this.

## Amendment 6 (Sat 04:5x, before any gate16 row): how the gate arm gets its switches
- **The first gate16 process (04:45:51) stopped at its own assert, with no row.** `cli.gpu` strips the SHIP_MTP keys
  from every stage's environment, and BIGMOE_MAXK is one of them (bt_b12's MTP config). So the stage saw neither
  BIGMOE_MAXK nor BIGMOE_EXT_BUILD.
- **Fix:** `cli.gpu` applies EVALSUITE_STAGE_ENV (comma-separated KEY=VALUE pairs) after its pops. Runner 31 passes
  `EVALSUITE_STAGE_ENV=BIGMOE_MAXK=160,BIGMOE_EXT_BUILD=build_next` for the gate arm only. live16b still asserts both,
  and GATE_N 160, at load.
- **The ref2b arm is complete (36/36, 03:34-04:40)** and is not re-run. Its rows came from pin b89f5bc24aab. The gate
  arm runs under the new pin, and the cli.py change touches only how the stage environment is built.
