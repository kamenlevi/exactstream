## Rules
- **`b12llfx2` PASSES if:**
  - all 45 rows are token-identical to `b12llfx`, AND
  - every process's lifetime peak footprint is ≤ 11.95 GB.
- **`b12llff2` PASSES if:**
  - all 45 rows are token-identical to `b12llff`, AND
  - every process's lifetime peak footprint is ≤ 11.95 GB.
- **Footprint:** the larger of the process's lifetime peak (`ri_lifetime_max_phys_footprint`) and its rows' sampled
  maxima, as in R-41C.
- **Identical tokens need no score test.** If `b12llff2` passes, it inherits R-41C part B's score result (rule 1), and
  fold-fast's memory condition is judged on `b12llff2`'s processes.
- **Follow-ups, fixed now:**
  - **A differing row:** its first differing token goes to Breakthrough and NewIdeas.
    - For `b12llfx2`, that would mean the fix is not exact on that row.
    - For `b12llff2`, a score test would be a new plan.
  - **Memory over the line:** the per-row footprints (row start, sampled max, lifetime) go to Breakthrough and Fit12.

**Information only, no pass line:**
- per-row footprints against the R-41C rows: creep per row and drops;
- decode tok/s and prefill seconds against the R-41C rows (unpaired);

## Procedure
- **Code:**
  - The new arms go into `cli` / `model` only after runner 27 (R-41C part B) has ended, never while it runs.
  - New variant names keep `b12llfx` / `b12llff` pinned to c3f957cba6.
- **Pre-flight:** before the verdict rows, each arm decodes one row (agent/bash17) under a scratch name (`.r9`) to
  confirm that its switches and bt_b12 sha load. These are not verdict rows, and the result discloses them.
- **Order:**
  - If FFX has passed and its env is recorded when the runner starts, `b12llff2` goes first (decision 5's open
    question), then `b12llfx2`.
  - Otherwise `b12llfx2` goes first, and `b12llff2` follows once its env is recorded.
- **Locks:**
  - Each process takes its own queued quiet lock. Leap's step 3x and Fit12's short screens interleave through the queue.
  - The agent process (about 15–19 minutes) is over the usual 15-minute chunk, by NewIdeas' plan, as in R-41C.
- **Runner:** `tools/day_runner28.sh`, detached; log `results/day28_runner.log`.
  - It pins the new bt_b12, the v3l / v3m engines, SpecDecode and MLX / mtplx, and stops (exit 7) if any of them
    changes.
  - It waits while PAUSE exists, `soft_end.sh` runs, the lid is closed with no external display, or less than 30 GB
    is free.
- **An agent process cut short:** its rows still count for tokens, and the 29-row process is re-run once, in full,
  under `.r1`, for the memory test.
