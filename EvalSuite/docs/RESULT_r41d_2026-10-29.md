## What it means
- **Decision 5 (fold-fast) is not settled by R-41D.** Fold-fast with the fix and the C_m 60 cap keeps its tokens,
  but a 29-row agent process still peaks 0.52 GB over the line, mostly through one row's transient.
  - For scale (arithmetic, not measured here): fold-fast's pools go 6.0 → 7.34 GB for C_m 51 → 64, ~0.10 GB per
    C_m step, so 0.52 GB is ~5 C_m steps.
  - Options for Breakthrough / NewIdeas: the v3o engine Breakthrough is checking (its bar: creep ≤ 3 MB per request),
    a look at write14's transient (it also lifts fold-exact by ~0.7 GB), or a lower cap on short prompts.
- **Fold-exact + the fix (`b12llfx2`) passes.** It can replace `b12llfx` as the 12 GB line's exact default (NewIdeas'
  call; its plan then moves the line to v3n + `BIGMOE_M01=1`, R-48). It lowers the math peak by 0.71 GB and leaves
  0.43 GB of headroom in the 29-row agent process.
- **Still open, for both arms:** whether one process levels off over many more requests, as a long-running server
  would serve them. The fixed arms creep less, and fold-exact's agent starts stop rising over the last 11 rows, but 29
  rows don't show the long run.
- **For the 45 GB route:** nothing; this is the 12 GB line's memory.
