- **A main-track gain, bit-identical:** the parts of tonight's prefill fix that keep the output exact cut the 24 GB track's

## Tonight's findings (details: ~/NewIdeas/GAPS.md, PLAN_B.md, DEAD.md)
1. RAM truth: MLX's "active memory" and process RSS both understate; all numbers now use Activity Monitor's figure.
2. Prefetch (falsified): predicted +11-14 %, measured +0 % per pass. Predicted reads were ~40 % wasted and used up the SSD's
   idle time. Side result: a forecast taken at each layer's start is the most accurate measured (82 % of a layer's experts
   vs 73 % for the forecast in use); precision, not volume, is what any future prefetch needs.
3. Prefill: long prompts fixed (~440 tok/s within 12 GB, quality checked on needles up to 16K, a 10K tool log and a real
   agentic turn: same accuracy, 13 of 14 answers identical, 3x faster). Short tool outputs (50-300 tokens) still take ~3-5 s
   because even a short prompt touches a third of the experts and the SSD is already at its limit while reading them.
   The exact cuts tested this morning are small (a GPU rebuild was slower; skipping experts already in memory saves at
   most ~12 %). The remaining lever is a slightly approximate prefill of short tool outputs; its quality on real agentic
   turns is being probed (Chat1000).
4. Long context: with the drafter, B12 fits 12 GB up to roughly 16-20K tokens of context; 32K takes 12.9 GB (was 19.1)
   and 64K 14.6 GB. The peak at long context is the attention step during prefill plus the stored context (~1.6 GB at
   64K). Getting 64K under 12 GB needs 8-bit storage of the context (new code, after the verdict) or a smaller expert
   cache at long context (a quality trade).
5. Cache: the memory freed by the prefill fix buys a ~10 % bigger expert cache within 12 GB (56 instead of 51 per
   layer): 5 % closer to the exact model on hard text but only ~2 % faster per verify pass (the larger gains first
   seen came partly from different output text). Because the cache is sized at load and
   your sessions run long, the default stays at 51 (12 GB up to ~16-20K tokens of context); 56 becomes a named setting
   for short chats. Waited reads fall only slowly with cache size. A frequency rule beats LRU by 7 % on waited reads;
   a future-knowing cache would save 38 %.
6. MLX 0.32.2 bug (TrainLab): one routine silently skips output rows beyond 32,767 routed rows per call. MTPLX guards it;
   some 35B agentic tests were affected and re-run (direction unchanged); no 125B result was affected.
7. Mac mini: modelled ~21-24 tok/s for B12 + drafter (its SSD makes every waited read ~4x longer); 50-75 needs ~3x fewer
   bytes per token, a quality trade. Probes queued at low priority.

  exact mode stays as it is either way.

  outputs). My recommendation: NOT now. FastMTP's gain (1.2x -> 1.8x) came mostly from teaching a one-step head to chain its
  guesses; the 125B's head already chains well (first 1/2/3/4 guesses right 85 / 71 / 61 / 51 %), so what is left is adapting
  it to your kind of text, maybe +10-20 %. Tonight's careful attempt lost 4.6 %, and the 125B's own outputs as training data
  cost ~8-10 GPU hours per million tokens here (FastMTP used ~100 million). The GPU pays more on SSD waiting and prefill.
