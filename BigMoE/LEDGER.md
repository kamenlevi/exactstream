**Definition of "exact" (Kamen, 2026-10-18):** our own **deterministic, batch-invariant** kernel set is the
reference. (1) It gives the same result every run, at any batch or verify size, streaming on or off
(kernel-band style, SpecDecode §5.4). (2) It is validated once against MLX eager: the logit gap at every
divergence must be a near-tie, and GSM8K-150 answers must be identical to the eager model. (3) After
that, every fast path (MTP and lookup speculation, streaming, prefetch) must be **bit-identical to the
reference**. (4) Where it's cheap, fuse while reproducing MLX's bf16 rounding points, so those kernels
stay bit-identical to eager as well. Each fusion this newly allows gets its own row below.
Practical consequence: decode (S=1) and verify (S=2..4) must run the same kernels. Either pad S=1 into
the verify band, or use kernels whose per-row result does not depend on M.

Ceilings: one token reads ~4.7 GB (≈3.4 dense, of which the hyper-connection mixers are **1.27 GB in
bf16** in this pack, + 1.33 routed) → **~17 ms at 280 GB/s ≈ 59 tok/s** for single-token decode. Beyond
that only with >1 token per pass (speculation). Current: ~45 ms/token → **~28 ms of overhead to attack**.
