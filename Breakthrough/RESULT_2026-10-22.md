## What changed today (all measured, all in SCOREBOARD.md)
- Prefill: TrainLab's decoupled prefill is the default (3x faster than the RAM-safe chunked prefill, 10.9 vs 11.5 GB;
  Chat1000's paired long-context check: 13/14 answers identical, same accuracy). Optional streaming: 435 tok/s.
- MTP no longer costs extra memory: the MTP layer's prefill runs grouped (-1.4 GB), its head is fed per chunk (-0.7 GB
  at 32K), and the state is not copied.
- Decode weights: B12 now keeps each expert's exact top-10 weight instead of renormalising: hard-text KLD -9 % on the real
  decoder, no speed or RAM cost (the variant the suite is judging).
- Repetition guard: ported (it was missing on this path).
- Main track: NewIdeas' dh_w4 (wait for ranks 0-3, the rest half a layer late) on v3's 3-bit tiers at C=128 passes all
  four per-part quality targets on the real decoder (IFEval is the tight one).

## Tested and dropped
Prefetching experts ahead (no gain), reading the next layer's predicted experts during prefill (+40-48 % slower at
typical sizes; prefill of small calls is SSD-bound), a GPU rebuild path for small calls (slower), skipping resident experts
in prefill (at most ~12 % of bytes), switching chunk sizes by context (-26 % prefill for 0.4 GB).
